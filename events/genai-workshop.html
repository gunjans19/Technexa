About The Workshop

The GenAI Masterclass at TECHNEXA 2026 takes participants from LLM fundamentals to deploying custom AI agents and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines using LangChain, OpenAI APIs, and HuggingFace.

Key Takeaways

  • Building RAG applications with vector databases (ChromaDB / Pinecone).
  • Fine-tuning open-source LLMs (Llama 3 & Mistral).
  • Building Autonomous AI Agents with LangGraph & CrewAI.
  • Industry certificate of participation for all attendees.
