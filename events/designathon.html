About

What is Designathon?

Designathon is a fast-paced UI/UX design sprint where creativity meets usability. Teams receive a real-world design problem and have 6 hours to ideate, wireframe, prototype, and present a complete design solution. Tools like Figma, Adobe XD, or Framer are welcome.

📋 Rules

  • Teams of 2–3 members. All members must be students.
  • Problem statement revealed at start of event.
  • Any prototyping tool allowed (Figma recommended).
  • No pre-built templates or past designs allowed.
  • Final submission: Figma/XD file + 5-min presentation.
  • Judging on design quality, usability, innovation, and presentation.

⚖️ Judging Criteria

Visual Design (30%)
Aesthetics, layout, colour, typography
Usability (30%)
User flows, accessibility, intuitiveness
Innovation (20%)
Creative solutions to the problem
Presentation (20%)
Clarity and persuasiveness of pitch
No coding required! This is purely a design event. Proficiency in Figma or similar tools is recommended.
