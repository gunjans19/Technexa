About RoboWar

RoboWar is a bot combat championship where teams design and build radio-controlled battle robots to fight in a controlled arena. The last bot standing wins! Robots will fight in head-to-head elimination matches judged on aggression, control, and damage.

🔧 Robot Specifications

  • Maximum bot weight: 15 kg
  • Maximum dimensions: 60cm × 60cm × 30cm
  • Power supply: 12V DC maximum (battery operated)
  • Autonomous operation: NOT allowed — must be RC controlled
  • Weapon systems: pushers, flippers, spinners allowed. No projectiles.
  • Flame-based weapons: NOT allowed
  • All sharp edges must be covered during inspection

📋 Combat Rules

  • Matches are 3 minutes long. KO wins immediately.
  • If no KO, judges decide winner based on aggression, damage, control.
  • Robot must pass safety inspection before competing.
  • No modifications to the robot after matches begin.
  • Teams get 15 minutes between matches for repairs.
  • Double-elimination bracket format.
