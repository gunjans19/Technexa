About Robo Race

Robo Race challenges engineering teams to design custom wired, wireless, or autonomous RC robots built to conquer an adrenaline-pumping obstacle track featuring gravel, bridges, hairpin turns, and speed stretches.

Track & Bot Specifications

  • Bot Dimensions: Max 30cm x 30cm x 30cm.
  • Weight Limit: Max 5kg.
  • Control: Wired/Wireless (RF, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi).
  • Time trial laps with penalties for track boundaries.
