About the Event

What is HackNova?

HackNova is the crown jewel of TECHNEXA — a non-stop 24-hour hackathon where teams of 2–4 members race against the clock to build innovative solutions to real-world problems. Fuelled by caffeine, creativity, and code, participants go from idea to working prototype in a single night.

Whether it's an AI-powered app, a blockchain solution, an IoT device, or a groundbreaking web platform — HackNova celebrates every form of innovation. The best ideas get seed funding connections and direct exposure to industry mentors.

📋 Rules & Regulations

  • Teams must have 2–4 members. Cross-college teams are allowed.
  • All code must be written during the hackathon. Pre-built components/libraries are allowed.
  • Projects must be submitted via the portal before the 24-hour mark.
  • Plagiarism or use of pre-existing projects will lead to disqualification.
  • Participants must carry their college ID and registration confirmation.
  • All team members must be present during final presentations.
  • Judges' decisions are final and binding.
  • Midnight food and beverages will be provided. Stay respectful.
Format

Event Rounds

Round 1 — Idea Submission (Online)
Abstract & Problem Statement
Submit a 300-word abstract of your proposed solution. Shortlisted teams proceed to the hackathon venue.
Round 2 — 24H Hackathon (Offline)
Build Phase — Sept 11, 9:00 AM
24 hours of non-stop building. Mentors available throughout. Midnight fuel provided.
Round 3 — Final Presentations
Demo Day — Sept 11, 10:00 AM
5-minute pitch + 3-minute Q&A before a panel of industry judges. Top 3 projects announced.

⚖️ Judging Criteria

Innovation (30%)
Originality and creativity of the solution
Technical Depth (25%)
Complexity and quality of implementation
Impact (25%)
Real-world applicability and scalability
Presentation (20%)
Clarity, pitch quality, and demo effectiveness
