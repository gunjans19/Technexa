General
TECHNEXA is the annual flagship technical festival of our college — a 3-day celebration of technology, innovation, and talent featuring 20+ events, workshops, competitions, and expert talks.
TECHNEXA 2026 is scheduled for September 11–17, 2026, at our college campus. All major events will be held at various venues across the campus.

Yes! TECHNEXA is open to students from all colleges and universities across the country. Outstation participants may register and attend all open events.
Registration
Registration for TECHNEXA events is completely free! Workshops are also free for all registered participants. There are no hidden charges.
Visit the Events page, choose your desired event, and click "Register Now." Fill in the form with your details and you'll receive a confirmation email. Simple as that!
Yes, you can register for multiple events as long as the event timings don't conflict. Check the schedule page to plan your participation.
The last date to register for most events is September 15, 2026. Paper Presentation abstract submission closes October 1, 2026. HackNova idea submission closes October 5, 2026.
You can update team member details until 1 week before the event. Cancellations can be made via email to technexa@college.edu. Spot registrations may be available for some events.
Events
There is no limit on the number of events. However, practically, you'll be limited by time conflicts. We recommend planning your schedule carefully using the Schedule page.

Workshops
Most workshops are designed for beginners to intermediate levels. Basic programming knowledge is helpful for technical workshops. The UI/UX Figma workshop requires no coding skills.
Yes! All participants who complete the workshop will receive a digital certificate of participation co-signed by TECHNEXA and the workshop speaker.
Bring your laptop with the recommended software pre-installed. Software requirements will be shared 1 week before the event via email. All materials will be provided digitally.
Logistics
Limited accommodation is available on campus for outstation participants on a first-come-first-served basis. Please email technexa@college.edu to request accommodation when registering.
Yes! Food and beverages will be provided during HackNova (24 hours). Lunch will be available at subsidized rates for all participants. Midnight snacks during HackNova are free.
Carry your college ID card, registration confirmation email (print or digital), and laptop with charger. HackNova participants should also bring sleepwear/pillow as they may need to stay overnight.
Prizes
The total prize pool for TECHNEXA 2026 is ₹5 Lakhs+, distributed across 20+ events. The flagship HackNova event has the highest prize pool of ₹50,000.
Cash prizes are awarded during the Valedictory Ceremony on Day 3. Certificates are sent digitally within 7 days of the event. Equipment prizes are handed over at the ceremony.
